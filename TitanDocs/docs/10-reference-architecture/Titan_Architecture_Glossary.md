# Titan Architecture Glossary

Defines short canonical meanings for major Titan terms.

## Terms

### Signal
A structured intent or event entering Titan’s validation and governance pipeline.

### Envelope
A bounded package of live decision context.

### Manifest
A structured map of domain boundaries, objects, rules, and tools.

### Slice
A bounded domain subset for one task or agent.

### Tool
An inspect, write, or external action surface invoked through governed execution.

### Overlay
A policy layer applied across domains without redefining core specs.

### Spool
The replay, recovery, and reconstruction subsystem.

### Sentinel
The readiness and domain approval layer.

### AEGIS
The governance and authority layer.

### Mode
A distinct operational working surface such as Jobs, Comms, Finance, Admin, or Social.

### Handoff
A bounded transfer of context and allowed action from one mode to another.
